Women’s Hormonal & Metabolic Consultation

Irregular cycles, persistent adult jawline breakouts, androgenic hair thinning, and unexplained weight changes are interconnected physiological manifestations of endocrine rhythm shifts.

In our Satya Nagar centre, our doctor looks at your cycle patterns, metabolic history, and dermatological symptoms in unity. When a gynecologist or endocrinologist is required for imaging or specialist management, we guide you transparently.

✦ The Care Methodology

Our 4-Stage Holistic Evaluation Protocol

01

Cycle & Symptom Chronology

A detailed review of cycle regularity, symptom onset, PMS severity, previous medications, and any past pelvic ultrasound scans.

02

Dermal & Trichological Corroboration

Physical inspection of androgen-sensitive skin zones (jawline, chin) and scalp density to gauge systemic hormonal impact.

03

Targeted Procedural Interventions

Non-invasive dermatological care (salicylic peels, hair growth factors) paired with nutritional advice to support hormonal equilibrium.

04

Coordinated Specialist Care

Whenever investigations indicate gynecological pathology or thyroid dysfunction, we coordinate transparently with specialist colleagues.

✦ Patient Clarity

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this consultation replace seeing a gynecologist?
No. This consultation evaluates how hormonal dysregulation manifests across your skin, hair, and metabolic energy, providing integrative support. When diagnostic pelvic scans or gynecological interventions are indicated, we coordinate referrals to specialist gynecologists.
Can hormonal acne and hair thinning be addressed simultaneously?
Yes. Both conditions often stem from androgen sensitivity or insulin resistance in conditions like PCOS. The doctor formulates a dual-action plan that calms dermal inflammation while stimulating hair follicle anchorage.
