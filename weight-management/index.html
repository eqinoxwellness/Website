Frequently Asked Questions

Why does Equinox avoid rapid weight loss programs?
Rapid weight loss through extreme starvation diets almost always burns precious muscle tissue and water, slowing down your resting metabolic rate. Once normal eating resumes, rebound fat gain occurs quickly. We focus on preserving muscle and burning visceral fat gradually for sustainable long-term health.
Do you prescribe weight loss pills or fat burners?
No. We do not use commercial fat burners, unregulated powders, or dangerous stimulants. Our protocols rely on medical assessment, metabolic nutrition, physical activity pacing, and gentle homeopathic systemic support.
How does insulin resistance affect weight?
When cells become resistant to insulin, the pancreas produces higher levels of insulin to clear blood glucose. High circulating insulin acts as a biochemical lock on fat cells, preventing lipolysis (fat breakdown) particularly around the abdomen. Addressing insulin sensitivity is key to unlocking stubborn fat.
