Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Laser Hair Regrowth machine work?
The Laser Hair Regrowth machine utilizes low-level laser photobiomodulation (LLLT). The focused light stimulates micro-circulation around the scalp's vascular beds, boosts cellular ATP energy synthesis in follicular roots, helps halt active shedding, and encourages healthier, thicker hair shafts.
What is Alsavique hair therapy from Italy?
Alsavique is an imported clinical hair restoration protocol from Italy. It combines biomimetic signal peptides, oligoelements, and vascular revitalizers micro-infused directly into the scalp to nourish weakened hair roots and support follicular density without irritation or greasy residue.
What is the key difference between PRP and GFC?
While both are derived from your own blood, GFC (Growth Factor Concentrate) is an advanced second-generation system. It isolates pure, pre-activated growth factors without red or white blood cells, resulting in significantly higher biological consistency and virtually zero post-injection soreness compared to conventional PRP.
Can treatments grow hair on completely bald, shiny areas?
No medical biotherapy can revive follicles that have irreversibly scarred over or atrophied into shiny skin. Our consulting doctor uses digital trichoscopy to inspect whether viable, miniaturized follicles remain before recommending any treatment.
