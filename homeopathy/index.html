Clinical Clarity

Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers regarding our homeopathic methodology and appointment process.

Can I take homeopathy alongside my current conventional medicines?

Yes. Homeopathic remedies can be safely integrated alongside conventional allopathic medicines in most cases. You should inform the doctor of every medication you currently take, and we strictly instruct that you never stop or alter any prescribed treatment without speaking directly to your treating physician.

How long is the initial consultation?

The first visit typically lasts between 45 to 60 minutes because the doctor conducts a thorough investigation into your medical history, family background, lifestyle patterns, and constitutional factors.

How frequently are follow-up visits scheduled?

Follow-up consultations are typically spaced 2 to 4 weeks apart, depending on whether the concern is acute or long-standing. The doctor explains the review cadence during your initial visit so you know what to expect.

Can remedies be prescribed over WhatsApp?

No. Remedies are prescribed strictly after a formal clinical consultation with our doctor. WhatsApp is reserved for scheduling, visit coordination, and pre-consultation inquiries.

Can chronic skin or hair issues be supported with homeopathy?

Yes. With 14 years of clinical experience, our consulting doctor frequently evaluates individuals experiencing recurring skin flare-ups and chronic scalp conditions where constitutional balance is indicated alongside dermatological care.
