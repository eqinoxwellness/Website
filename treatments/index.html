Comprehensive Procedural Specifications

Every procedure at Equinox is preceded by a thorough diagnostic assessment of medical history, lifestyle factors, and skin phototype (Fitzpatrick III–V).

1. Pico & Q-Switched Nd:YAG Laser (1064nm)

Photomechanical Precision

The gold standard for treating pigmentary dyschromia in Indian skin. Instead of relying on heat (photothermal effect), ultra-short picosecond and nanosecond pulses generate powerful acoustic shockwaves (photomechanical effect) that fragment stubborn melanosome clumps into microscopic dust without collateral thermal damage.

Primary Uses: Melasma, carbon facial, hyperpigmentation, tattoo removal
Anesthesia: None required (chilled air applied)
Session Duration: 25 to 35 minutes
Recovery: Zero social downtime; mild pinkness for 1–2 hours

2. Fractional Micro-Needle RF (MNRF)

Dermal Collagen Matrix

Precision gold-coated micro-needles penetrate smoothly to calibrated depths between 1.5mm and 3.5mm into the reticular dermis. Bipolar radiofrequency pulses create controlled micro-zones of thermal coagulation, breaking down fibrotic anchor bands under pitted acne scars and triggering profound neo-collagenesis for skin tightening and anti-aging.

Primary Uses: Pitted acne scars (boxcar & rolling), skin tightening, anti-aging, open pores
Anesthesia: Topical numbing cream (30–40 mins prior)
Session Duration: 45 to 60 minutes
Recovery: Mild redness and pinpoint swelling for 24–48 hours

3. Laser Hair Regrowth Machine (Scalp Biostimulation)

Micro-Circulation & Regrowth

Low-level laser photobiomodulation (LLLT) calibrated at therapeutic 650nm wavelengths. The focused photonic energy penetrates into the scalp's vascular beds, significantly improving micro-circulation surrounding depleted hair follicles, stimulating cellular ATP synthesis, and supporting hair fall reduction and active hair regrowth.

Primary Uses: Scalp micro-circulation, hair fall control, follicular thickening & regrowth
Anesthesia: Completely non-invasive, no anesthesia needed
Session Duration: 25 to 30 minutes
Recovery: Zero downtime; return to daily schedule immediately

4. Autologous PRP & GFC (Growth Factor Concentrate)

Cellular Trichology

Derived from the patient's own blood platelets through advanced centrifugation and separation technology. GFC isolates pure, concentrated active growth factors (PDGF, VEGF, bFGF, EGF) free of unneeded cellular debris, micro-infused directly around thinning hair follicles to prolong the active anagen growth phase.

Primary Uses: Pattern hair thinning, telogen effluvium, crown density restoration
Comfort: Minimal pinprick sensation with topical scalp prep
Protocol: 4 to 6 sessions spaced 3–4 weeks apart
Recovery: Zero downtime; resume normal routine same day

5. Alsavique Hair Restoration Therapy (Imported from Italy)

European Trichology Formulation

A specialized clinical hair restoration protocol directly imported from Italy. Formulated with biomimetic signal peptides, trace amino acids, and micro-vascular stimulants, Alsavique deeply infuses the follicular root zone to protect against oxidative cellular stress and promote structural hair shaft density.

Primary Uses: Weak roots, severe hair thinning, post-stress shedding, scalp revitalisation
Source: Certified premium import from Italy
Protocol: Multi-session doctor-customized cycles
Recovery: Clean, non-greasy finish with zero downtime

6. Skin Boosters, PDRN (Salmon DNA) & Skin Vector Therapy

Cellular Biorejuvenation

Polydeoxyribonucleotide (PDRN) derived from salmon DNA is a biocompatible cellular activator that stimulates rapid DNA tissue repair, enhances dermal capillary circulation, and upregulates fibroblast activity. Combined with Skin Vector structural bioremodeling and hyaluronic skin boosters, this modality restores deep physiological hydration and elasticity.

Primary Uses: Dull crepey texture, dehydrated skin, micro-lines, compromised dermal barrier
Anesthesia: Topical numbing cream
Session Duration: 35 to 45 minutes
Recovery: Tiny superficial blebs that settle within 12–24 hours

7. Diode Laser Hair Reduction (808nm)

Sapphire Chill Technology

The 808nm diode wavelength is universally recognized as the safest and most effective standard for hair reduction on Indian skin types (Fitzpatrick IV–V). Its deep penetration bypasses epidermal melanin to deliver thermal coagulation selectively to the follicular bulb and germ cells, while an integrated sub-zero sapphire chill tip protects the outer skin.

Primary Uses: Facial hair, underarms, arms, legs, full body reduction
Comfort: High comfort with continuous contact sapphire cooling
Protocol: 6 to 8 sessions scheduled 4–6 weeks apart
Recovery: Zero downtime; apply sunscreen and avoid direct sun for 48h

8. Advanced HydraFacial (Vortex Infusion & Extraction)

Medical-Grade Hydro-Dermabrasion

A 4-step medical treatment using patented vortex fluid technology: deep lymphatic pore cleansing, gentle salicylic and glycolic acid resurfacing, painless vacuum extraction of comedones and sebum plugs, followed by saturated infusion of hyaluronic acid, peptides, and antioxidant botanical extracts.

Primary Uses: Blackheads, congested pores, uneven surface texture, pre-event glow
Comfort: Gentle, soothing, and relaxing sensation
Session Duration: 40 to 50 minutes
Recovery: Immediate clarity and radiant glow with zero peeling

9. Clinical Skin Tone & Melanin Regulation Treatment

Physician-Supervised Depigmentation

A medical protocol combining physician chemical peels (mandelic, lactic, ferulic, kojic), topical tyrosinase inhibitors, and low-fluence laser toning. Designed to gently balance uneven skin tone, diffuse sun tanning, and regulate excessive melanocyte activity safely without post-inflammatory rebound.

Primary Uses: Uneven tone, sun tanning, superficial pigmentation, dull complexion
Safety: Tailored strictly to Indian skin phototypes to avoid PIH
Protocol: 3 to 5 sessions combined with medical at-home barrier regimen
Recovery: Mild microscopic shedding over 3–4 days

10. Comprehensive Clinical Acne & Scar Treatment

Inflammation Control & Scar Remodeling

Doctor-led diagnosis separating bacterial acne vulgaris from hormonal cystic acne or fungal folliculitis. Management follows a phased protocol: first active flare suppression and comedone clearance using medical salicylic peels and topical actives; followed by subcision and Fractional MNRF to remodel deep acne scars.

Primary Uses: Active acne flares, blackheads, post-acne marks (PIH/PIE), boxcar & ice-pick scars
Assessment: Digital dermal analysis plus hormonal / dietary screening
Integration: May be paired with constitutional homeopathy for chronic recurrence
Recovery: Gradual, steady clinical improvement with doctor follow-ups
