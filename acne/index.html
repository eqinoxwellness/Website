Doctor-Led Clinical Acne & Scar Protocol

Acne is rarely a simple surface condition. It is a multi-factorial follicular inflammatory cascade influenced by sebum viscosity, hormonal signalling, bacterial biofilms, and genetic barrier integrity.

In our Satya Nagar consultation suite, our consulting doctor conducts a meticulous microscopic evaluation of your breakouts, differentiating active pustular lesions from post-inflammatory erythema (PIE) and deep atrophic scars before formulating a progressive clinical roadmap.

✦ The Clinical Pathway

Our 4-Stage Diagnostic & Care Protocol

A structured medical roadmap that addresses acute flare-ups first, followed by epidermal cellular clearance and deep dermal collagen rebuilding.

01

Diagnostic Intake & Microscopy

We examine skin under polarized light, evaluate follicular keratinization, review past topical reactions, hormonal triggers, and lifestyle variables.

02

Active Flare De-escalation

Targeted medical-grade exfoliating peels and non-comedogenic sebum-modulating topical protocols are introduced to extinguish bacterial inflammation.

03

Sub-Dermal Scar Remodeling

For pitted rolling or boxcar scars, Fractional Micro-Needle RF (MNRF) delivers heat directly to the reticular dermis to break fibrotic bands and trigger neo-collagenesis.

04

Barrier Fortification & Maintenance

Long-term stratum corneum repair regimen with gentle lipid replenishers and broad-spectrum photoprotection to prevent future recurrent flares.

✦ Technology & Science

Advanced Procedural Modalities

Depending on whether your primary concern is active inflammatory papules or chronic textural scarring, the doctor may discuss specific precision in-clinic modalities:

Micro-Needle RF (MNRF) Scar Remodeling

Gold-insulated micro-needles penetrate 1.5mm to 3.5mm into the reticular dermis, discharging fractional radiofrequency energy directly where scar fibrosis resides, while sparing the superficial epidermis.

Clinical Dermatological Peels

Lipophilic Salicylic, Mandelic, and Azelaic acid formulations calibrated to penetrate deep inside the sebaceous follicle, unblocking micro-comedones and clearing persistent redness.

HydraFacial Vortex Pore Extraction

Gentle fluid-vortex vacuum extraction removes congestive debris and infuses targeted salicylic & hyaluronic acid solutions without manual squeezing or trauma.

✦ Patient Clarity

Frequently Asked Questions

How soon can I expect to see an improvement in active acne?
Skin cells renew over an approximately 28-day cycle. Active inflammatory breakouts typically begin de-escalating within 2 to 4 weeks of starting medical-grade care, while deep tissue scar remodeling with MNRF continues to produce new collagen for 3 to 6 months post-treatment.
Will MNRF cause extended downtime or peel my skin?
Unlike ablative CO2 lasers that vaporize the top layer of skin, MNRF uses insulated micro-needles that deliver thermal radiofrequency directly into the deep dermis, leaving the surface layer intact. Expect mild erythema (redness) resembling a light sunburn for 24 to 48 hours, with minimal social downtime.
Should I stop using my current creams or facewash before visiting?
No. Keep using your regular skincare and bring the containers with you. Inspecting what you have been using helps the doctor diagnose whether any comedogenic or harsh active ingredients are triggering flare-ups.
How many MNRF scar remodeling sessions are generally advised?
Most patients with moderate atrophic scarring undergo a protocol of 3 to 4 sessions, spaced 4 to 6 weeks apart. The doctor evaluates the depth of your scar tissue during your initial assessment and provides a realistic session estimate before booking.
