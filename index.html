How a first visit works

Four steps, and you decide at the end. Nothing is booked unless you ask for it.

  1. Message or call

    Tell us your name and what you would like to talk about. We reply during clinic hours, usually within minutes.

  2. Three short questions

    We ask what the concern is, how long it has been going on and what you have tried, so the doctor can prepare.

  3. The consultation

    The doctor examines, asks about your history and explains what is likely going on, in plain language.

  4. You decide

    You hear the options, what each involves and what it costs. You can start, think it over, or ask for a referral.

Who you will see

Your consultation is directly with our consulting doctor, not a sales executive.

Every first visit follows the same order: listen, examine, explain, and only then talk about options. You are welcome to ask questions, take notes, or bring someone with you.

Questions people ask before booking

Do I need an appointment?

Yes, booking an appointment in advance ensures you are seen promptly without waiting. Message us on WhatsApp or call, and we will offer you a couple of convenient time slots.

Is the clinic open on Fridays?

No, the clinic is closed on Fridays. We are open 11am to 8pm from Saturday to Thursday.

Where exactly is the clinic?

The clinic is located on the first floor of Plot No. 69, Kali Mandir Road, Satya Nagar, Bhubaneswar 751007, directly above Sweekruti Creations.

How much does a consultation cost?

We tell you the consultation fee on WhatsApp or over the phone before you book, so there are no surprises at the desk. If any in-clinic procedure or course of sessions is discussed after the assessment, the doctor explains the cost per session clearly before you decide.

Will I get a specific result?

No, no clinic can promise a specific outcome because skin, hair and health respond differently from person to person. What we do provide is a thorough clinical assessment, an honest explanation of what to expect, and a structured plan before you agree to anything.

Who will I see?

Your consultation is directly with our consulting doctor. If your concern needs another specialist, we will tell you honestly and help with the referral.

Can I get a same-day appointment?

Yes, same-day consultation slots are often available if you contact us earlier in the day. Send a quick message on WhatsApp or call us to check current availability.
