✦ The Photomedicine Protocol

Our 4-Stage Pigmentation Care Roadmap

A methodical medical protocol that identifies pigment depth before initiating targeted non-ablative clearance.

01

Dermal Depth Mapping

Under specialized cross-polarized light, we map whether your pigment is superficial (epidermal), deep vascular-associated (dermal), or mixed melasma.

02

Photoacoustic Particle Breakdown

Targeted 1064nm laser pulses fire in picosecond fractions, generating acoustic stress waves that pulverize melanosomes without overheating epidermal tissue.

03

Enzymatic Tyrosinase Modulation

In-clinic micro-infusions of Tranexamic Acid and Kojic bio-compounds suppress the tyrosinase enzyme to block fresh melanin synthesis.

04

Photoprotective Cellular Barrier

Formulation of a customized maintenance regimen with physical mineral sunscreens and antioxidant shields to prevent rebound hyperpigmentation.

Energy & Medical Modalities

Different forms of pigmentation demand distinct wavelengths and treatment mechanics:

Pico & Q-Switched Nd:YAG 1064nm Laser

The gold standard for pigmented lesions in Asian Fitzpatrick skin types III–V. Operates at a deep-penetrating 1064nm wavelength that bypasses surface melanin to target stubborn dermal pigment beds.

Tranexamic Acid Targeted Mesotherapy

Direct micro-droplet delivery of clinical Tranexamic Acid into hyperactive pigment spots to inhibit plasmin activity and down-regulate vascular melasma pathways.

Mild Non-Ablative Brightening Peels

High molecular weight Mandelic and Lactic peels provide smooth superficial exfoliation of pigmented keratinocytes without inducing inflammatory heat.

✦ Patient Clarity

Frequently Asked Questions

Can melasma be resolved in a single session?
Medically, no. Melasma is an ongoing vascular and hormonal melanocyte disorder triggered easily by UV light, heat, and hormonal shifts. While Pico laser and topical protocols can significantly disperse and lighten pigment clusters, long-term control requires consistent medical sun protection and periodic maintenance.
Will laser toning cause burns or make my pigmentation darker?
When calibrated correctly by an experienced doctor at non-thermal acoustic settings (such as our 1064nm laser toning protocol), the energy fractures pigment clusters through mechanical stress rather than thermal coagulation, minimizing the risk of post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation.
How many sessions are typically required for noticeable clearance?
Superficial sun spots often respond within 2 to 3 sessions. Deeper mixed melasma typically requires 4 to 6 sessions spaced approximately 3 to 4 weeks apart to achieve gradual, safe cellular lightening without skin irritation.
