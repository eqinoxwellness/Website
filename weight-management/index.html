✦ Discipline: Metabolic Health & Sustainable Physiology

Physician-Supervised Weight Management

Sustainable weight loss is not about starvation or crash diet fads. It requires understanding individual basal metabolic rates, visceral adipose distribution, and hormonal drivers.

In our Satya Nagar centre, our doctor assesses your baseline metabolic health, lipid markers, and daily schedule to build a gradual, medically supervised roadmap that preserves lean muscle tissue while reducing visceral fat.

✦ The Metabolic Roadmap

Our 4-Stage Clinical Care Protocol

01

Body Composition Intake

Assessment of visceral fat, skeletal muscle ratio, basal metabolic rate (BMR), and current activity routines.

02

Metabolic Biomarker Evaluation

Review of fasting blood glucose, HbA1c, lipid profile, and thyroid function to detect insulin resistance or metabolic slowdown.

03

Calibrated Nutritional Roadmap

A customized plan designed around locally available foods that balances macronutrients without extreme caloric deprivation.

04

Ongoing Physician Check-ins

Fortnightly reviews to adjust caloric intake as your metabolism adapts, ensuring consistent sustainable progress.

✦ Patient Clarity

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you prescribe commercial slimming pills or injections?
No. We do not prescribe unregulated fat burners or commercial slimming cocktails. Our approach is grounded in physiological metabolic regulation, insulin stabilization, and sustainable nutritional pacing supervised by our doctor.
How frequently are review consultations scheduled?
Follow-up visits are typically scheduled every 2 to 3 weeks to evaluate body composition changes, check adherence, and calibrate dietary recommendations as your metabolic rate shifts.
