Our Consulting Doctor & Clinical Philosophy

At Equinox, you never sit across from a commissioned sales representative or corporate beauty consultant. Every consultation is conducted directly with our consulting doctor in Satya Nagar.

Our consultations follow an unhurried, four-stage clinical order: listen deeply to your history, inspect skin and follicular structures under high magnification, explain the biological mechanisms in plain terms, and only then discuss evidence-based procedural or medical options.

✦ Practice Standards

Our Core Medical Commitments

Standards designed to protect patient trust and uphold Indian medical advertising ethics.

01

No Hurried Sales Pitches

You sit in a private, calm consultation room. We will never push commercial package deals or force you to make an impulsive decision on your first visit.

02

Transparent Explanations

Every option is explained with realistic clinical expectations, session timelines, downtime requirements, and precise upfront fees before anything commences.

03

Honest Referrals

If an acne breakout is deeply cystic requiring oral systemic management or an irregular cycle stems from complex gynecological factors, we assist with immediate specialist referrals.

04

Supportive Companions Welcome

You are warmly encouraged to bring a family member, partner, or trusted friend into the consultation room, ask questions, and take notes.
