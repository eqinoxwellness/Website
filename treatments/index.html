✦ Complete Clinical Portfolio

Comprehensive Procedural Specifications

Our consulting doctor assesses each patient's skin phototype (Fitzpatrick III–V) and medical history before determining which technology is appropriate.

1. Pico & Q-Switched Nd:YAG Laser (1064nm)

Photomechanical Precision

The gold standard for treating pigmentary dyschromia in Indian skin. Instead of relying on heat (photothermal effect), picosecond and nanosecond pulses generate powerful acoustic shockwaves (photomechanical effect) that fragment stubborn melanosome clumps into microscopic dust without collateral thermal damage.

Primary Uses: Melasma, freckles, sun spots, post-acne marks
Anesthesia: None required (cooling air applied)
Session Duration: 20 to 30 minutes

2. Fractional Micro-Needle RF (MNRF)

Reticular Collagen Synthesis

Precision gold-coated micro-needles penetrate smoothly to calibrated depths between 1.5mm and 3.5mm. Once positioned inside the reticular dermis, bipolar radiofrequency pulses create micro-zones of thermal coagulation. This triggers profound neo-collagenesis and breaks deep fibrotic anchor bands under pitted acne scars.

Primary Uses: Pitted acne scars, enlarged pores, skin laxity
Anesthesia: Topical numbing cream (30 mins prior)
Recovery: Mild redness for 24–48 hours

3. Autologous GFC (Growth Factor Concentrate)

Cellular Trichology

Derived from the patient's own blood platelets through advanced separation technology, GFC isolates pure active growth factors (PDGF, VEGF, bFGF, EGF) completely free of unneeded red or white blood cells. Delivered directly to the scalp's follicular matrix to prolong the anagen active growth phase.

Primary Uses: Pattern hair thinning, telogen effluvium
Comfort: Minimal pinprick sensation
Protocol: 3 to 6 sessions spaced 3–4 weeks apart
