Frequently Asked Questions

Honest medical answers about our consultations, equipment calibration, and appointment protocols.

What qualifications and experience does the consulting doctor have?

Our consulting physician brings 14 years of clinical experience across constitutional homeopathy, clinical cosmetology, and aesthetic dermatology. Every consultation is conducted directly with the doctor in an unhurried, private suite.

What is PDRN cellular therapy and Skin Vector therapy?

PDRN (Polydeoxyribonucleotide) utilizes salmon DNA fragments to trigger fibroblast activation, accelerating skin barrier repair, collagen synthesis, and deep cellular hydration. Skin Vector therapy works along structural facial tension vectors to restore firmness without artificial volume.

How does the Laser Hair Regrowth machine work?

The Laser Hair Regrowth machine delivers targeted low-level laser light energy (LLLT) to the scalp. This stimulates micro-capillary circulation around dormant follicles, increases cellular ATP production, and aids in reducing active hair shedding while thickening existing shafts.

What is Alsavique Italian hair restoration therapy?

Alsavique is an advanced hair and scalp revitalizing formulation imported directly from Italy. It delivers biomimetic growth factors, specialized amino acids, and micro-nutrients directly to the follicle matrix to improve density and hair resilience.

How do you book a child counselling session?

For child and adolescent supportive counselling, we request parents or guardians to call our clinic desk (+91 63725 28534) beforehand. This allows our doctor to understand the child's situation and confirm whether our supportive setting is suitable.
