Frequently Asked Questions

Can I take homeopathic remedies while on allopathic medicines?
Yes. Constitutional homeopathy is administered safely alongside your ongoing conventional prescriptions (e.g. thyroid, diabetes, or blood pressure medicines). We never ask you to discontinue any prescribed allopathic medication.
Why does the first consultation take 45 to 60 minutes?
Constitutional analysis requires understanding the individual as a whole: previous illnesses, sleep architecture, appetite, reactions to weather, and personal stressors. This detailed picture allows the doctor to identify your specific constitutional remedy.
Are laboratory tests reviewed during the visit?
Yes. Please bring your recent blood panels, imaging reports, or specialist prescriptions. Our doctor reviews all medical documentation to understand your complete clinical baseline.
