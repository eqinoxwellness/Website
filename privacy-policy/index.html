Privacy Policy

How Equinox Aesthetic & Wellness Centre handles your personal data, appointment details, and browsing privacy in strict compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP), 2023.

1. Clinical Data Protection Commitment

Equinox Aesthetic and Wellness Centre (Plot No. 69, 1st Floor, Kali Mandir Road, Satya Nagar, Bhubaneswar, Odisha 751007) operates under doctor-patient confidentiality. We collect only the minimum required information to coordinate appointments and respond to your direct inquiries.

2. Information We Collect

When you request an appointment callback through our web forms or WhatsApp desk, we collect your name, mobile phone number, preferred time slot, and the clinical discipline or treatment concern you indicate.

Strict Healthcare Advertising Rule: We never share medical history, clinical concerns, or consultation topics with third-party ad platforms or data brokers.

3. Consent & Telemetry

Optional analytics telemetry (Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity) runs solely to help us understand page navigation and optimize usability. You may choose to browse without cookies or manage your consent choices at any time.

4. Contact Desk

For any questions regarding your information or to request data removal from our front desk records, please reach our administrative team at +91 63725 28534 or visit our clinic suite in Satya Nagar during consultation hours (11:00 AM – 8:00 PM).
