Patient Clarity

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is Pico Laser safer than traditional lasers for Melasma?
Traditional Q-switched or heat-based lasers heat the skin significantly. In Indian skin types, excess heat can trigger reactive melanogenesis, causing rebound hyperpigmentation. Pico lasers deliver energy in trillionths of a second (picoseconds), shattering melanin with photomechanical acoustic waves with minimal thermal transfer.
What does a Carbon Laser Facial involve?
A layer of liquid carbon is applied across the face to penetrate micro-pores. When the laser passes over the carbon, it gently vaporizes the particles, lifting away blackheads, sebum, and dead surface cells while tightening pores and creating an instant porcelain glow.
How many sessions are needed for tattoo removal?
Tattoo clearance depends on ink depth, density, chemical composition, and age. Most amateur or professional tattoos require several sessions spaced 6 to 8 weeks apart to allow the body's immune macrophages to clear the shattered pigment safely.
