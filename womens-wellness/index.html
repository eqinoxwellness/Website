Frequently Asked Questions

Why does PCOS cause adult jawline acne?
In PCOS, elevated ovarian or adrenal androgens increase sebum production and alter sebum viscosity along the lower third of the face (jawline and chin). This thicker sebum blocks follicular openings and creates an anaerobic environment for acne bacteria.
Will you prescribe oral hormonal contraceptives?
Our clinic emphasizes lifestyle pacing, metabolic nutrition, and constitutional balance. When oral contraceptives or specialized hormonal therapy is indicated, we refer you directly to licensed gynecologists and endocrinologists.
Can I bring previous sonography or blood test reports?
Yes, absolutely. Please bring your pelvic ultrasound reports, lipid profiles, and hormone assays to help the doctor understand your complete clinical baseline.
