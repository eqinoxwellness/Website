Transparent Referral & Crisis Notice

Equinox Aesthetic & Wellness Centre operates an outpatient consultation room. We do not provide acute crisis intervention or inpatient psychiatric care. If an individual presents with severe clinical depression, psychosis, bipolar conditions, or thoughts of self-harm, our doctor provides an immediate, transparent referral to licensed psychiatrists and tertiary healthcare facilities.

Immediate Emergency Assistance: In case of acute emotional distress or emergency crisis, please call 112 or contact the national Tele-MANAS toll-free helpline at 14416 immediately.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens during a mental health counselling session?
Sessions are unhurried (45 minutes) dialogues in a private room. Our doctor listens to your concerns, examines somatic manifestations (sleep patterns, headaches, fatigue), and helps formulate a practical, structured routine adjustment without judgment.
How does child counselling work at Equinox?
We request parents to speak with the doctor prior to booking so we can understand the developmental context. Sessions for children and adolescents are gentle, calm, and pressure-free, focusing on emotional coping tools, study pressures, and interpersonal difficulties.
Is the consultation kept completely confidential?
Yes. All conversations are maintained under strict clinical confidentiality within our medical suite.
