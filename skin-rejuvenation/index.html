Frequently Asked Questions

What is PDRN and how is it derived?
PDRN (Polydeoxyribonucleotide) consists of DNA fragments extracted from salmon germ cells. Salmon DNA is remarkably compatible with human DNA. In the dermis, it acts as a molecular building block for tissue repair, stimulating natural cell renewal and collagen production without allergic rejection.
Do Skin Boosters add artificial volume like dermal fillers?
No. Dermal fillers are heavily cross-linked gels designed to physically sculpt volume (e.g. cheekbones or lips). Skin Boosters are micro-fluid hyaluronic acid formulations that distribute evenly across the dermal bed solely to attract water and improve hydration, elasticity, and radiance.
How does HydraFacial fit into this protocol?
Advanced HydraFacial acts as an ideal preparatory and maintenance treatment. By clearing dead stratum corneum cells and congested pores with gentle vortex fluid extraction, it allows cellular active ingredients like PDRN and boosters to penetrate with maximum efficacy.
