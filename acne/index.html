Sub-Dermal Scar Remodeling

1. Fractional Micro-Needle RF (MNRF)

Gold-insulated micro-needles penetrate 1.5mm to 3.5mm into the reticular dermis, discharging bipolar radiofrequency pulses directly where scar fibrosis resides. Breaks down tethered scar bands and triggers profound neo-collagenesis for skin tightening, scar remodeling, and anti-aging.

Primary Uses: Boxcar & rolling acne scars, skin tightening
Anesthesia: Topical numbing cream (30–40 mins)
Recovery: Mild erythema settling within 24–48 hours

Frequently Asked Questions

How does MNRF treat pitted acne scars?
MNRF (Micro-Needling Radiofrequency) uses insulated gold micro-needles to bypass the epidermis and release radiofrequency heat directly into the reticular dermis. This controlled thermal coagulation breaks the fibrous tethering bands beneath boxcar and rolling scars, stimulating fresh collagen and elastin synthesis to lift the skin.
Will chemical peels cause severe peeling or downtime?
No. Our peels use superficial-to-medium depth formulations (mandelic, salicylic, lactic) that cause microscopic exfoliation rather than intense visible peeling. You can continue daily work with proper moisturization and sunscreen.
Can adult hormonal acne be managed with constitutional homeopathy?
Yes. With 14 years of clinical experience, our doctor often pairs external clinical dermatological clearing with individualized constitutional homeopathy to stabilize recurring systemic and hormonal flare triggers safely.
