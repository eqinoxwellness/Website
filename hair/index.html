✦ The Trichological Pathway

Our 4-Stage Follicular Revitalization Protocol

A physician-guided roadmap designed to halt excessive shedding, clear scalp inflammation, and awaken dormant follicular units.

01

Digital Trichoscopy Scan

High-magnification dermoscopic examination of the scalp to calculate follicular unit density, identify miniaturized vellus hairs, and inspect peripilar inflammation.

02

Systemic & Blood Profiling

Review of serum ferritin, vitamin D3, thyroid status, and sex-hormone binding globulin (SHBG) to rule out internal drivers of excessive shedding.

03

Autologous GFC Cellular Therapy

Your blood is processed in specialized gel-separation tubes to isolate pure recombinant growth factors (PDGF, VEGF, EGF) delivered directly to the dermal papilla.

04

Anagen Phase Maintenance

Prescription topical vasodilators, anti-androgen serums, and medical nutritional protocols to anchor and sustain strengthened hair shafts over time.

✦ Cellular Trichology Technologies

Advanced Clinical Modalities

Depending on the diagnosis from digital trichoscopy, our doctor will explain candidate modalities:

Growth Factor Concentrate (GFC)

A state-of-the-art advancement over conventional PRP. GFC releases high concentrations of autologous growth factors directly from blood platelets without red or white blood cells, minimizing post-injection soreness.

Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) Trichology

Enriched plasma containing autologous bioactive proteins micro-injected around thinning hair follicles to boost micro-vascular supply and stimulate cellular metabolism.

Clinical Scalp Clarifying Protocols

Medical salicylic scalp exfoliation and anti-fungal botanical infusions for chronic seborrheic dermatitis, clarifying the follicular ostium for healthy hair emergence.

✦ Patient Clarity

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the key difference between PRP and GFC?
While both use your body's own platelets, GFC (Growth Factor Concentrate) is an advanced next-generation system that pre-activates and extracts pure growth factor proteins into an acellular fluid. This eliminates unneeded red/white blood cells, resulting in higher consistency, less post-procedural soreness, and superior cellular stimulation.
Can GFC or PRP grow hair on completely bald, shiny scalp areas?
No. Cellular growth factors work by reviving and thickening existing miniaturized hair follicles. If a follicle has scarred over or completely atrophied (leaving shiny, follicle-free skin), medical bio-therapies cannot regenerate it. A digital trichoscopy assessment allows the doctor to tell you with complete transparency whether viable follicles remain.
How many sessions are typically required before seeing results?
Hair growth follows a 3-month biological cycle. Significant reduction in daily shedding is commonly observed after 2 sessions (weeks 4 to 8), while increased shaft thickness and density improvement become visible after 3 to 4 sessions.
