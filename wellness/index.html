The 4-Stage Wellness Consultation

A structured framework examining how physical habits, stress response, and metabolic status intersect.

01

Lifestyle & Baseline Audit

A deep dive into your sleep hygiene, circadian rhythm, energy troughs throughout the day, dietary timing, and physical exertion parameters.

02

Stress & Somatic Assessment

Evaluation of physical stress manifestations—including tension headaches, gastrointestinal irritability, resting heart rate elevations, and emotional wear.

03

Practical Restorative Plan

Formulation of realistic, step-by-step adjustments to routine, nutritional pacing, and evidence-grounded non-pharmacological lifestyle interventions.

04

Transparent Referral Pathway

If your symptoms indicate clinical depression, severe anxiety, or psychiatric conditions, we provide an immediate and transparent referral to qualified specialists.

Supportive Consultations & Scope

Clinical Clarity

Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers about our general wellness and supportive counselling appointments.

What takes place during a wellness consultation?

A wellness consultation is a structured, unhurried dialogue reviewing your daily routines, energy rhythms, sleep hygiene, and physiological stress responses. You receive practical, realistic guidance and a defined pathway forward.

Is what I discuss kept private?

Yes. All conversations between you and the doctor are maintained in strict clinical confidentiality within our medical consultation room.

When would the doctor refer me to another specialist?

Whenever symptoms indicate clinical depression, acute anxiety, bipolar tendencies, or require specialized psychiatric pharmacotherapy, we provide a transparent, prompt referral to a licensed psychiatrist or clinical psychologist.

Do I need a diagnosed medical condition to book?

No. Many individuals schedule visits simply for a preventive lifestyle evaluation, stress audit, or to discuss strategies for improving daytime energy and sleep quality.

Do parents need to call before booking child counselling?

Yes. We ask parents or guardians to call the clinic desk prior to booking child appointments so we can understand the nature of the concern and verify that our outpatient setting is the most appropriate starting point.
