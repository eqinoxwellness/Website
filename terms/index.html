Clinical consultation guidelines and website terms for Equinox Aesthetic & Wellness Centre, Satya Nagar, Bhubaneswar.

The content provided on this website is for informational and educational awareness regarding the clinical disciplines and treatment modalities available at Equinox Aesthetic and Wellness Centre. It does not replace a comprehensive, in-person clinical assessment conducted by a qualified doctor.

2. Medical Evaluation & Responses

Individual biological responses to aesthetic and wellness modalities vary based on tissue biology, skin phototype, endocrine status, and adherence to post-care regimens. We do not make commercial promises or outcome claims without a clinical diagnostic baseline.

3. Emergency Services Notice

Equinox is an outpatient consultation and procedural aesthetic clinic. We do not provide 24-hour emergency casualty services. In the event of an urgent medical crisis, call 112 immediately. For mental health support, contact the Tele-MANAS helpline at 14416.
